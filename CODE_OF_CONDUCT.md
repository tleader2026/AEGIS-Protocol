# Code of Conduct

AEGIS contributors are expected to maintain a professional, respectful, and technically rigorous environment.

We welcome participation from researchers, implementers, public institutions, infrastructure providers, independent auditors, and standards practitioners.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, bad-faith disruption, and disclosure of private security reports before coordinated handling.

Maintainers may moderate issues, pull requests, and discussions to preserve a constructive standards process.
